About me

Julia Winogrodzka

I am an attorney registered with the Lublin Bar Association under number xxxxxxxxxx. I earned my law degree from the John Paul II Catholic University of Lublin and completed my legal clerkship with the Lublin District Bar Council, which culminated in my passing the bar exam.

I began gaining professional experience while I was still in college, working with a law firm specializing in criminal law. I am constantly improving my skills, including by completing courses on personal data protection and mediation.

In my work, I prioritize professionalism, effectiveness, and a personalized approach to every case. I am fully committed to supporting my clients in resolving legal issues, providing reliable assistance, clear communication, and a sense of security at every stage of our collaboration.
